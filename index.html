About

I am a software engineering researcher working on multimodal large language models, multimodal entity linking, and continual learning. My work focuses on making large models better at using visual information, connecting evidence across modalities, and adapting to new knowledge.

I am interested in research that can move from strong model ideas to useful, efficient systems.
